# Rebrand: "SecondLife" → "Ikea Used Furniture"
### Trademark risk assessment, brand inventory and migration plan

Prepared 22 Oct 2026 for https://www.ikeiausedfurniture.ae. This document goes with [IKEA-SEO-AUDIT.md](IKEA-SEO-AUDIT.md) and [ikea-keyword-database.xlsx](ikea-keyword-database.xlsx).

> **This is not legal advice.** It is a summary of public evidence to help you decide whether to involve a UAE IP lawyer. Before using "IKEA" in the business's own name, get written advice from a lawyer, or written permission from the trademark owner.

---

## 1. Verdict

**Don't launch "Ikea Used Furniture" as the store name without written permission from IKEA's trademark owner (Inter IKEA Systems B.V.).**

- **Legal risk is high.** IKEA's own published terms require prior written permission for any use of the IKEA name. UAE law lets the owner of a registered or well-known mark stop unauthorised use of an identical or similar mark. IKEA actively enforces its mark against business names and domains, including in 2024.
- **SEO benefit is small.** Google ranks pages on how relevant they are to the query, not on the brand name. The IKEA product and model terms that bring traffic are already in your titles, headings and copy (branch `seo/ikea-quick-wins`). Adding "| Ikea Used Furniture" to every title mostly repeats a word Google already sees. It doesn't unlock rankings you can't get otherwise.
- **It works against you on Google's site name.** Google says a site name should be "unique", "accurate" and "not misleading", and that generic names are "unlikely to be selected". "Ikea Used Furniture" is both generic and suggests an IKEA connection.

**Recommended path (option B below):** keep a non-IKEA brand and use IKEA *descriptively*. That's already how the branch is written: "Used IKEA Furniture in UAE – Beds, Daybeds & Sofa Beds | SecondLife". If you still want the IKEA name, follow option A (ask for permission) first. I have **not** changed the brand in the code.

| # | Evidence | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **IKEA's terms.** The IKEA Life at Home terms say IKEA name trademarks may not be used without prior written permission; the rights belong to Ingka Holding B.V. or Inter IKEA Systems B.V. ([lifeathome.ikea.com/terms-of-use](https://lifeathome.ikea.com/terms-of-use/)). Using it as your *name* for furniture is the exact case these provisions cover |
| 3 | **Enforcement history.** 2014: IKEA sent a cease-and-desist to the IKEAhackers.net fan blog over its name and domain. The founder kept the domain only by removing all advertising ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jeremy-quittner/ikea-hackers-sent-cease-and-desist.html), [analysis](https://www.ellacheong.asia/ikea-v-ikeahackers-the-trade-mark-dispute-that-never-was/)). IKEA has won several WIPO domain cases, including one where swapping a letter was found to be an obvious misspelling of the mark ([WIPO D2016-0052](https://www.wipo.int/amc/en/domains/decisions/text/2016/d2016-0052.html), [D2013-0387](https://www.wipo.int/amc/en/domains/decisions/text/2013/d2013-0387.html)) | IKEA watches names and domains that contain IKEA or look like it, and acts on them |

| 5 | **Local context.** IKEA is sold in the UAE through official stores (franchise partner Al-Futtaim). A shopper who sees "Ikea Used Furniture" in search results, on WhatsApp or on a delivery van could reasonably think it's an IKEA service | This is the "likelihood of confusion" that trademark claims rest on |

### What *is* generally lower-risk

Describing genuine products accurately: "We sell used, genuine IKEA furniture", "Used IKEA MALM bed", product titles that name the model. Reselling genuine branded goods and naming them truthfully is widely accepted (often called nominative or descriptive use), provided you:

- sell only genuine IKEA items under IKEA wording,
- don't use IKEA's logo, fonts, blue-and-yellow colours or photography,
- don't imply endorsement, and
- keep the "independent, not affiliated with IKEA" statement prominent.

The site already does most of this. Even descriptive use can be challenged, so confirm the details with your lawyer.

## 3. Your existing domain: `ikeiausedfurniture.ae`

You haven't asked to change the domain, and **I don't recommend migrating it now.** A migration costs rankings and the domain is the site's indexed identity. But you should know about the risk:

- "ikeia" is a one-letter variation of IKEA plus a descriptive phrase. `.ae` domains are covered by the aeDRP, run by aeDA (part of TDRA), and complaints go to WIPO ([aeDRP rules](https://centralnicdomains.com/ccTLDPolicies/AE/Rules%20for%20the%20Domain%20Name%20Dispute%20Resolution%20Policy%20for%20.ae.pdf), [example decision](https://wipo.int/amc/en/domains/decisions/pdf/2023/dae2023-0009.pdf)). A complainant has to show the domain is confusingly similar to the mark, that you have no legitimate interest, and bad faith. One registrar's explainer says .ae may only need bad-faith registration *or* use, not both ([aeserver](https://www.aeserver.com/ae-domain-disputes-wipo/)). That claim is unconfirmed.
- In domain disputes, genuine resellers have sometimes kept domains containing a brand when they meet the "Oki Data" tests: they really sell the branded goods, sell **only** those goods on the site, clearly disclose that they aren't affiliated, and don't try to corner the brand's domains. Your site passes on disclosure. **The "IKEA Hydraulic Ottoman Bed" may fail the "only genuine goods" test** if its frame isn't IKEA. That's another reason to fix its naming (audit item T3).
- Calling the *business* "Ikea Used Furniture" on top of this domain makes all of these factors look worse. Keeping a distinct brand helps show you're a reseller, not an imitation.

**Low-cost safeguard:** register a neutral domain for your brand (for example `secondlife-furniture.ae` or a new brand's `.ae`) and point it at the site with a redirect. That way you have a fallback if you ever have to move. Don't move the site now.

Clearance checks for any finalist:
1. UAE Ministry of Economy trademark search (classes 20 and 35).
2. Your licensing authority's trade-name check (DED or the relevant free zone).

5. A Google search for confusingly similar UAE businesses.

Avoid Swedish-sounding names, blue-and-yellow colours or IKEA-style typography. They would undo the point of choosing a neutral name.

## 7. Google site-name markup (for whichever name is approved)

Google reads the `WebSite` markup from the **home page** and prefers it over other signals. It also uses the `<title>`, `og:site_name` and headings, so these must all match. The branch already outputs this from `siteConfig.name`. After approval, add `alternateName`:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "WebSite",
  "@id": "https://www.ikeiausedfurniture.ae/#website",
  "name": "<Approved brand>",
  "alternateName": ["<Short form, if genuinely used>", "ikeiausedfurniture.ae"],
  "url": "https://www.ikeiausedfurniture.ae/"
}
```

- Google's documented fallback is to list the lowercase domain last in `alternateName`.
- The current code uses `url: siteConfig.url` without a trailing slash. Google asks for the canonical home page; the homepage canonical is also slash-less, so this is consistent. If you change one, change both.
- Homepage title pattern: `Used IKEA Furniture in UAE – Beds, Daybeds & Sofa Beds | <Brand>`. IKEA stays descriptive and the brand stays distinct.
