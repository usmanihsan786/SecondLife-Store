**Generated:** 2026-10-23 · **Depth:** quick scan · **Method:** competitor-profiling skill, alternative stack (browser, curl and WebFetch). **No SEO data source was connected**, so traffic, domain rank, keywords and backlinks are *unavailable*, not estimated.

## Landscape
- **Who wins the clicks:** Google's map pack and community marketplaces (Facebook groups, dubizzle).
- **Specialist sites:** few and weak. readymyspace.com is the closest model, with priced used-IKEA product pages. Two exact-match used-furniture sites were unreachable on the scan date.
- **IKEA.com:** ranks with Last Chance / offers pages, but it serves new-product intent.

## Takeaways
1. Your advantage is **service plus trust** (92 reviews, delivery plus installation). No organic competitor states both.
2. The organic gap is **priced, indexed product pages for used IKEA models**, which readymyspace demonstrates.
3. The rival map-pack listings copy the IKEA-name tactic, so the name alone isn't a durable moat. Reviews and real service information are.
