# Image Placement Guide

Put your photos in the folders below. The website shows every photo in a variant's folder, in filename order.

- Suggested filenames: `01.webp`, `02.webp`, `03.webp`, `04.webp` (add `05.webp`, `06.webp` … for more).
- `01.webp` is the main photo, used on product cards.
- Formats accepted: .webp (best), .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .avif.
- Recommended: landscape photos in a **4:3 shape**, about **1600 × 1200 px**. Other shapes are cropped to fit, never stretched.
- Any folder without photos shows a neutral "Photo coming soon" placeholder, so nothing breaks.
- After adding photos, rebuild/redeploy the website so they are picked up.

All paths below are inside your project folder.

## Other images on the site

**Your logo mark** (required)  
`/public/images/brand/mark.png`: a square PNG (512 × 512 px) with a transparent background. The store name is written next to it automatically (see OWNER-GUIDE.md, section 11).

**Link-preview image** (required)  
`/public/images/brand/share.png`: 1200 × 630 px, shown when a page without its own photo is shared.

**Homepage hero photos** (automatic)  
The hero shows the first photo of three real items from your stock (set in `heroPicks` in
`src/components/home/Hero.tsx`), each linking to that exact item. Sold-out items are skipped. The styled room images
`/public/images/hero/hero.webp` and `condition.webp` are no longer used: they did not show your own stock.

**Homepage condition section photo**  
Uses `/public/images/cards/hemnes-bed.webp`, one of your own stock photos (`CONDITION_IMAGE` in
`src/components/home/Sections.tsx`).

**Category tiles and guide cards**  
Category photos are set in `src/data/options.ts` (`categories.*.image`); guide card photos in `src/data/guides.tsx`
(`cardImage`). Use your own photos only.

**Shop by series cards** (optional)
